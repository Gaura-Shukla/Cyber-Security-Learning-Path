IMPORTANT TECHNICAL INTERPRETATION

A TCP SYN followed by RST, ACK commonly indicates that a TCP
connection attempt was rejected because no service was listening
on that port, or that the connection was otherwise actively rejected.

If Nmap reports a port as closed and the corresponding RST response
is observed, those findings are consistent.

This does not establish that all target ports are closed.
Only four specified TCP ports were tested in the first experiment.


CURRENT KNOWLEDGE TO REINFORCE

1. Nmap -sT selects a TCP Connect Scan.
2. -p specifies the ports to scan.
3. TCP SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK represents the normal connection handshake.
4. TCP RST, ACK commonly indicates rejection of a connection attempt.
5. Nmap states open, closed, and filtered have different meanings.
6. An open port is not automatically a vulnerability.
7. Wireshark observes packet traffic; Nmap interprets scan responses.
8. ARP and DHCP have different purposes.
9. A packet capture may contain host traffic as well as VM traffic,
   depending on the capture interface and VirtualBox networking.
